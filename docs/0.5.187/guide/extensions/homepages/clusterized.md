# Clusterized (homepage)

> **Type:** Homepage · **Look:** one card per cluster

## What it shows

**Clusterized** is a **cluster-centric** landing dashboard: **one card per cluster**, each with a status indicator, live CPU/Memory, and **quick Magnify** access. It's the most practical homepage for **multi-cluster** operators — see every cluster's health at a glance and jump straight into one:

![Clusterized homepage — one card per cluster](../../../_media/guide/homepage-clusterized.png)

## Applying & configuring it

1. **☰ → Manage extensions → Homepages → Clusterized → Activate.** The home screen adopts it.
2. **Configuration:** when you activate a homepage, its **setup** may open, and afterwards its **card in the manager shows a ⚙️ gear** you can use to **reconfigure** it (e.g. which clusters/endpoints to show, refresh interval). *(Some homepages have no configuration.)*
3. **Deactivate** (from its card) to return to the default home.

## Notes

- Best paired with **multi-cluster** setups — see [Cluster management](../../admin/06-cluster-management).
